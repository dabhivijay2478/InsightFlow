# CEO and Legal Claims Review Register

This register prevents unverified statements from being presented as customer
commitments. It does not approve or reject a claim. Until CEO/Legal records an
owner, evidence, jurisdiction, and approval date, the public wording must remain
factual and non-promissory.

| Claim area | Evidence required before publication | Current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security certification (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA or similar) | Current certificate/report, scope, legal entity, systems covered, and expiry | Do not claim certification |
| Privacy-law compliance (GDPR, CCPA or similar) | Counsel review, data map, lawful basis, DPA/subprocessor terms, request workflow | Do not claim blanket compliance |
| Data residency or regional processing | Verified infrastructure regions, subprocessors, backup locations, and contractual controls | Describe only deployed topology |
| Retention and deletion guarantees | Implemented schedules, backup behavior, deletion verification, exceptions, and contract language | Describe configurable/observed behavior only |
| Availability, SLA, RTO, or RPO | Measured service history, exclusions, support process, and executed agreement | No numerical guarantee |
| Encryption claims | Verified algorithms, key ownership/rotation, data states covered, and exception inventory | State implementation scope precisely |
| Connector production certification | Automated contracts, live credential evidence, manual UI record, limitations, and responsible approver | Available Now is owner classification, not certification |
| Customer logos, records, run counts, or testimonials | Customer authorization and provenance | Use clearly labeled synthetic examples only |

## Publication rule

Roadmap material must be labeled Planned, Coming Soon, or In Development.
Automated test results may be reported as engineering evidence but must not be
converted into legal, compliance, uptime, or independent-certification claims.
